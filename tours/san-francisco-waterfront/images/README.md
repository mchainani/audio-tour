These photos are CC0 or public-domain works, as recorded on their linked Wikimedia Commons file pages. Source records are retained here even when attribution is not required. Files are saved locally and do not load from external image hosts.

Images were resized to a maximum 1600-pixel longest edge, JPEG-compressed, and oriented according to EXIF metadata. The carousel may crop a photo to fit its frame.
