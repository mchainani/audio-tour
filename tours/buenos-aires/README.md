## Route and visiting notes

This is a chapter sequence, not a continuous walking route or a timed guided navigation system. It preserves the supplied order, including returns from Recoleta Cemetery to El Ateneo and back to Pilar, and then south to Cueros Antílope before returning north to Palermo. Allow separate transport between distant districts. Tigre is a separate excursion in Buenos Aires Province and works well on another day. Visit duration and travel time are additional to narration time.

Chapter pins are approximate orientation points, not surveyed entrances or accessibility-certified routes. The Tigre delta pin is deliberately at the central river terminal on land; no particular boat route, boarding gate, or island is implied. Confirm the named address in Maps and follow current local access arrangements. Narration does not depend on being able to enter the stadium, cemetery, church, bookstore, or a private island.

No ticket, boat trip, admission, transport, or purchase is included with this content. Check current prices, hours, event restrictions, religious services, and access before visiting. The screenshots' admission labels were not carried over as promises. Floralis movement and the rose display are not guaranteed. Boat narration is intentionally not synchronized to route-specific landmarks.

## Editorial approach

The voice is an informed, welcoming English-speaking guide, with occasional natural Spanish terms and no invented personal biography. Chapters connect things to observe with historical context and everyday customs. Political violence and bereavement are discussed directly and respectfully. Indigenous and African-descended histories are included alongside European migration. Legends, brand statements, and interpretations are distinguished from documented chronology.

The narration is original prose, synthesized from the linked sources rather than copied or translated from them. Citations and production directions remain outside spoken text. The individual files in `transcripts/` are the source of narration for both the app and audio generator; edit those files when approving changes. Editorial notes are never intended for speech generation.

The user selected Microsoft Andrew Multilingual (`en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural`) at −5% rate, matching the existing Plaza de Mayo recording, and approved online speech generation for the remaining twelve chapters. Andrew is an American English voice; this selection supersedes the initial Argentine-accent direction. The transcripts are unchanged. The generic repository generator defaults to Ava, so set `NARRATION_VOICE=en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural` to retain Andrew when regenerating this tour's configured recordings.
