Our final stop is near the Manhattan pedestrian entrance to the Brooklyn Bridge. Opened in 1883, the bridge connects Manhattan and Brooklyn across the East River. Its stone towers and web of cables remain part of the city's familiar outline.

You do not need to cross the bridge to appreciate the idea behind it: separate places brought into conversation. Think back to the harbor where this tour began. New York is a city of connections, made visible in its ferries, streets, and bridges.
